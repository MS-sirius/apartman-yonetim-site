Deleting your account and data

Apartman Yönetim · developer: MS Sirius

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the Apartman Yönetim app.
  2. Open the Profile tab and tap Delete my account at the bottom.
  3. Read what will be deleted and what will be kept, enter your password and tap Delete my account.
  4. You are signed out. Your account is permanently deleted after 30 days.
If you change your mind, you can sign in with the same phone number and password within 30 days and tap Cancel deletion.
If you are a site manager, you must first hand over management to someone else (Manage › Team and roles).

If you cannot enter the app

Send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" to mssiriusapp@gmail.com. Include your full name, the phone number you sign in with and the name of your site. After confirming that the account is yours, we process your request and tell you the result within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your sign-in account: your password and sign-in details
  • Your roles in the sites and the link between your account and person records
  • Your notification keys and notification preferences

What is kept, and why

  • Your name and unit history (which unit you lived in or owned, and when)
  • Your dues, debt and payment records, receipts
  • The record of actions you took in the site (votes, approvals, audit log)

These records are part of the site's shared funds and dues ledger. They are kept by the site management because of the Condominium Law No. 634 and legal retention duties; even if your account is deleted, the accounts of the other unit owners must still add up. You can send a correction or deletion request about these records to the site management from Profile › My personal data or by email.
