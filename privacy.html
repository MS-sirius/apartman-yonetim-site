Privacy Policy

Version 4 · Effective date: 26 November 2026

This is an English translation for information. The Turkish text is the legally binding version.

This text explains how personal data is processed in the Apartman Yönetim Android app (package name com.mssirius.apartmanyonetim). It is also the privacy notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK); it is the detailed version of the notice inside the app.

1. Data controller and data processor

The data controller is the management of the site (building) you belong to. The site management decides which people are recorded and keeps the dues and funds records.

The data processor is MS Sirius, who develops and runs the app. Data is stored only on behalf of the site management and for the app to work; it is not used for any other purpose and is not sold.

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact: full name, phone, email
  • Unit details: the unit you are linked to, period of ownership or residence
  • Financial details: dues, debts and payment records, receipts
  • Activity in the site: requests, issue reports, notices, votes
  • Account details: the phone number you sign in with, your password in encrypted (irreversible) form, sign-in time
  • Device details: the notification key (token) of your device, used to send notifications
  • Usage data: the days you open the app, a random device ID created on this phone and anonymous usage counts (app version, language, platform, country). Platform means Android or iOS and its version, phone or tablet. The country comes from the phone's region setting; your location is not used. Kept only for usage statistics and deleted after 400 days.
  • Purchase details (site managers only): purchases of the support plan and the usage plan (sites above 30 units) — plan, date, amount, Google Play order number and the manager who bought it. Your card and payment details stay with Google Play and never reach the app or us.
  • Ad information: residents and unit owners may see an ad when the app opens (site managers and staff do not). For our own ads we only count, anonymously, how many times they are shown and tapped. Google's ads may use your device's advertising ID.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. It contains no third-party analytics.

3. Purpose

Tracking and collecting dues and shared expenses, keeping funds and budget records, notices and communication, tracking issues and tasks, carrying out owners' board decisions and votes, keeping your account secure and sending you notifications.

4. Collection method and legal basis

Data is collected electronically through the app, entered by you or recorded by the site management.

Legal basis: fulfilling obligations under the Condominium Law No. 634 (KVKK art. 5/2-ç) and the legitimate interest of the site management (KVKK art. 5/2-f). Therefore your explicit consent is not requested separately.

5. Who receives the data

  • The site management and people it appoints (assistant, auditor, accountant, board members) — only as much as their duties require. Other residents of the same site only see what the management shares (such as notices and funds summaries).
  • Server service (cloud hosting provider): data is stored on servers within the European Union (transfer abroad, KVKK art. 9).
  • Notification service (Google): the notification key and notification text are sent to Google so that notifications reach your device.
  • Payment service (Google Play): support plan and usage plan payments are taken through Google Play; purchase details are read from Google to verify the purchase.
  • Ad service (Google): when a Google ad is shown, your device's advertising ID and technical details are sent to Google to choose and measure the ad. Users in Europe are asked for consent first; consent can be changed in the app under Profile › Ads.
  • Authorised public bodies when required by law.

6. Security

All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored in irreversible form. Each user can only see the data their site and role allow; this rule is enforced on the server at database level.

7. Retention period

Your contact details are kept as long as you are connected to the site. Dues and funds records are kept with your name because of legal retention duties, even if you leave or ask for deletion.

If you delete your account, your phone, email, account and roles are deleted after a 30-day waiting period. Details: Account deletion.

8. Your rights (KVKK art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information, to learn the purpose, to know who it is transferred to, to request correction if incomplete or wrong, to request deletion where conditions are met, to object and to claim compensation for damage.

You can send your application to the site management in the app from Profile › My personal data. If you cannot enter the app, write to the address below. Your application is answered within 30 days at the latest.

9. Children

The app is not designed for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If this text changes, the version and date on this page are updated; the app shows the new text once at sign-in.
